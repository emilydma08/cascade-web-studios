Custom SB

Built for a handpainted skateboard business, this website was built as a complement to a pre-existing Etsy site and meant to showcase the artist's work and process. A sleek and modern look was prioritized.

VISIT WEBSITE ⨠

Project Overview

Features such as a moving gallery of skateboards, numerous videos showcasing the process of the artist, and a clear navigation path keep attention on the products and make the website more effective. Design choices such as the color scheme, font, and images reflect the style of the artist.
